# NYC Spots Map

A free, mobile-friendly map of places to go in and around New York City: landmarks, views, parks, museums, food, Michelin-starred restaurants, shows, classes, fireworks and parades, seasonal events, and day trips, plus layers for public restrooms and for free Wi-Fi and phone charging. Each place lists its cost, hours or season, a tip, and links to the sources used to check it.

- Tap a pin or a list item for details and directions.
- Allow location access to see a blue You are here dot and distances. Your location is used only in your browser.
- Filter by type, cost, or a search word.
- Turn on public restrooms with the WC button, or tap Nearest restroom. Restrooms come from the city's Public Restrooms dataset on NYC Open Data.
- Turn on free Wi-Fi and charging with the Wi-Fi button, or tap Nearest Wi-Fi. It shows working LinkNYC kiosks from the city's kiosk status data (free Wi-Fi, USB charging, free calls in the U.S.), public library branches, and two spots whose own pages confirm free Wi-Fi. This layer loads from wifi.json only when you turn it on.
- Dated events hide themselves after their last day.

Prices, hours, and dates were checked in October 2026, mostly on official sites. Confirm before you go.

Map data and tiles: OpenStreetMap contributors (https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). Mapping library: Leaflet (BSD-2-Clause) and Leaflet.markercluster (MIT); their licenses are in vendor/.
