Horse carriages in Central Park

Horse carriages line up inside Central Park at East Drive at Grand Army Plaza, Center Drive at Sixth Avenue, and West Drive at Seventh Avenue (Central Park Conservancy). Up to $72.22 for the first 20 minutes, then up to $28.89 for each extra 10 minutes, per carriage (city maximum since May 3, 2025); tip not included (city fare rules). Can you still take a horse carriage ride in Central Park?

Yes, for now. The City Council voted on Oct 22, 2026 to end horse-drawn carriage rides by June 1, 2028. Carriages line up inside the park at East Drive at Grand Army Plaza, Center Drive at Sixth Avenue, and West Drive at Seventh Avenue, per the Central Park Conservancy. The city's maximum fare is up to $72.22 for the first 20 minutes, then up to $28.89 for each extra 10 minutes, per carriage (city maximum since May 3, 2025); tip not included. Rides start at 10 AM on weekdays and 9 AM on weekends, and horses go back to the stable at 90 F or hotter and at 18 F or colder.
